About Golf Handicap

Golf Handicap is a personal handicap tracker for South African golfers. It calculates your handicap index using the official World Handicap System (WHS) formula, and helps you track every round you play.

What This App Does

  • Tracks all your golf rounds with accurate score differentials
  • Calculates your handicap index using the official WHS 2024 rules
  • Provides a course handicap calculator for any course or friend
  • Includes 400+ South African golf courses with official ratings
  • Backs up your data securely to the cloud

Important Notice

Golf Handicap is not an official handicapping service. Only GolfRSA, through the HNA system, can issue an official Handicap Index in South Africa. To get an official handicap, you must be a member of a golf club affiliated with GolfRSA.

This app is intended for personal tracking and reference. The calculations match the WHS formula, but the results are not recognized as an official handicap.

Data & Privacy

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase and is only accessible to you. We do not sell or share your data with anyone. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What We Collect

  • Account information: Your email address (when you sign up)
  • Golf data: Your rounds, scores, courses, and handicap information
  • Device data: Nothing — we don't track devices or IP addresses

How We Use Your Data

  • To calculate your handicap index using official WHS formulas
  • To sync your data across your devices (phone, tablet, computer)
  • To restore your data when you get a new phone

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore database), located in Johannesburg, South Africa. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Only you can access your own data — our security rules prevent anyone else from seeing your rounds.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party. Ever. The only exception is if required by law.

Your Rights (POPIA)

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data at any time
  • Correct any inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator

Data Deletion

You can delete individual rounds and courses directly in the app. To delete your entire account and all data, please contact us. We will permanently delete your account within 30 days.

Children

This app is not intended for children under 13. If we discover a child under 13 has created an account, we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any significant changes will be announced in the app. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, please contact the app owner.
